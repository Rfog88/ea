Last updated: May 30, 2026

Program name

EA Personal Assistant, operated by Andrew Fogle (sole proprietor).

Program description

EA Personal Assistant is a single-user personal assistant. It sends the account owner reminders, a daily summary of their own calendar and reminders, scheduled callback notifications, and replies to messages the owner sends to the Service. The account owner is the only recipient; no third parties are messaged and no marketing messages are sent.

How to opt in

The account owner opts in by entering their own mobile phone number when configuring the assistant during setup.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the owner's activity and requests — typically a few messages per day.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out

You can cancel messages at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, you will receive no further messages from the Service.
