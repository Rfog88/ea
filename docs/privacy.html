EA Personal Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

The EA Personal Assistant ("the Service") is a single-user personal assistant operated by Andrew Fogle (sole proprietor). This policy explains what information the Service handles and how it is used.

Who uses the Service

The Service has exactly one user: the account owner. No other person is enrolled or messaged. The owner opts in by entering their own mobile phone number when configuring the assistant.

Information we collect

  • The account owner's mobile phone number.
  • The content of SMS messages the owner sends to and receives from the Service (reminders, daily summaries, replies, and requests).
  • Basic delivery metadata (timestamps and delivery status) used to operate and troubleshoot message delivery.

How we use it

Information is used solely to operate the personal assistant for the account owner — delivering reminders, daily summaries, scheduled callbacks, and replies to messages the owner sends. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message content and phone numbers are transmitted only through the messaging carrier and infrastructure required to deliver the messages you have requested.

Marketing

The Service sends no marketing or promotional messages. All messages are personal-assistant notifications and replies requested by the account owner.

Opting out

The owner can stop all messages at any time by replying STOP, or get assistance by replying HELP.

Data retention & security

Message data is retained only as long as needed to operate the assistant and maintain a personal audit log, and is stored on access-controlled infrastructure.
